The subject policy in the repository's subject vocabulary register sets, per document type, the fewest (min) and most (max) concepts a document should carry. The rule counts the distinct concepts a document's prefixed subjects resolve to, ignoring plain keywords and unresolved subjects, and reports a count outside those bounds once, against the subject key. Below min, the message points to the githerd_subject_policy tool and the reference suggester. The type's policy may set the severity to info, warning or error.

## Trigger

The repository's subject policy, kept in the subject vocabulary register at
`extensions["githerd:subjectPolicy"]`, sets for each document type the fewest
(`min`) and most (`max`) concepts a document should carry. The rule counts
the distinct concepts a document's prefixed subjects resolve to and reports,
once, on the `subject` key:

- More than `max`. The built-in default is 5.
- Fewer than `min`. The built-in default is 0, so a repository opts in by
  raising it. A document with no `subject` key counts as zero.

What counts: each concept once, however it is spelt (`ability:traceability`
and `Ability:Traceability` are one), and a retired concept as the concept
that replaces it. Plain keywords and prefixed subjects that do not resolve
are not counted.

A document type without its own entry uses the policy's `default`; without a
policy, or with an invalid one, the built-in default applies (GHFM0016
reports the invalid policy).

## Why the rule exists

Too many subjects is tag spraying: a document filed under every concept is
found under none of them. Too few leaves a document invisible to the facet
and the roll-ups that group work by concept. The bounds keep the subject list
a short, honest statement of what the document is about.

## Fix

1. Above `max`: keep the concepts that say what the document is about and
   drop the rest, or move them to plain keywords.
2. Below `min`: `githerd_subject_policy` lists the concepts the type allows,
   and the vendored suggester
   (`schemas/vendor/subject-vocabulary/<version>/suggest/Suggest-Subjects.ps1`)
   proposes candidates from the document's text. It never writes them.

**Quotable fix.** Paste this into a review comment:

> **GHFM0017 (subject-count-outside-policy).** The subject policy bounds how
> many concepts this type of document carries. Trim the list to the concepts
> that say what the document is about, or add concepts the type allows; the
> subject suggester proposes candidates.

type: adr

  - ability:traceability
  - ability:governability
  - ability:discoverability
  - ability:observability

# Too Many Subjects

at most five.

## Enforcement

Default severity is `warning`. A type's policy may set its `severity` to
`info`, `warning` or `error`, which applies to this rule and GHFM0016 for
that type; `info` is reported at SARIF level `note`. The registry records the
rule as enforcement phase B. This rule postdates the front-matter standards
spec (design-038) section 6.2 phase listing.
