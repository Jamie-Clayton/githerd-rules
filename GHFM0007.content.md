## Trigger

The front matter carries a `title`, the body carries a level-one ATX heading,
and the two are the same string once trimmed. The comparison is
case-insensitive, so `Shared Heading` and `shared heading` count as a match.

## Why the rule exists

The same title stored in two places drifts the moment either one is edited
alone, and then every consumer has to decide which copy is authoritative. The
harness convention is that the body H1 wins, because it is the one a reader
sees and the one a reviewer notices when it is wrong.

This is the rule most often argued with, so the honest framing: it is not
saying a title is bad, it is saying two titles are. Keep one.

## Fix

Pick one of the two, then delete the other.

1. **Preferred.** Delete `title` from the front matter and let the body H1
   carry the name. Nothing in the harness requires `title` to be present.
2. **Alternative.** Keep `title` and make it genuinely different from the H1,
   for example a short catalogue label against a longer descriptive heading.
   Two strings that differ only by punctuation are still two titles.

**Quotable fix.** Paste this into a review comment:

> **GHFM0007 (title-duplicates-h1).** This document stores its title twice,
> once in front matter and once as the body H1, so the two will drift. Delete
> the front-matter `title` and keep the H1, which is the harness convention.
> If you need both, make the front-matter title a distinct short label rather
> than a copy of the heading.

## Example (violating)

Copied verbatim from `tests/fixtures/front-matter/ghfm0007-triggering.md`, so
the example is executable against the validator.

````markdown
---
type: spec
id: spec-hm007-fail
title: Shared Heading
---

# Shared Heading

Front-matter title duplicates the H1. GHFM0007 (title-duplicates-h1) must fire.
````

## Example (compliant)

Copied verbatim from `tests/fixtures/front-matter/ghfm0007-passing.md`.

````markdown
---
type: spec
id: spec-hm007-pass
title: Front Matter Title
---

# A Different Body Heading

Title differs from H1, so GHFM0007 must not fire.
````

## Enforcement

Default severity is `warning`, so the finding annotates a pull request without
blocking it. The rule is active from enforcement phase A, per the front-matter
standards spec (design-038) section 6.2, and no phase promotes it to `error`.

## Formerly

emits the `GHFM` identifier only, and the retired code survives as a
`deprecatedIds` entry in the SARIF output so an archived CI log still resolves
to this page.
